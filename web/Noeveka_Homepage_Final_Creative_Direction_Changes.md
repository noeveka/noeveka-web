Supporting copy: Independent advisory, practical architectures, and measurable outcomes that help enterprises create lasting value from data and AI.

Primary CTA: Book a Strategy Call →

Implementation notes:

Keep copy as HTML, not baked into the image.

Use the hero image as a background or media layer with Ajay positioned in the right 40-45%.

Keep the left 40% visually quiet so the HTML copy remains legible at all breakpoints.

Avoid futuristic AI graphics, neon networks, floating interfaces, binary code or hologram effects.

The visual should communicate senior advisory, architecture, judgement and enterprise context.

2. Hero image specification

3. Validate credibility claims

Before launch, validate every testimonial, statistic, customer identity, rating, technology relationship and experience claim. No dummy credibility content should reach production.

4. Technology strip label

Use: Experience across leading enterprise platforms

P1 - Strongly recommended before launch

1. Make “Why Noeveka” a signature editorial section

Replace the current standard feature-card treatment with large sequential numbering, stronger typography, more whitespace and fewer rounded containers.

Recommended scale: numbers 56-72 px, headings 24-30 px, supporting copy 16-18 px.

2. Replace generic differentiators

Remove: Dedicated Support, Professional Team

Enterprise-first thinking
Architecture shaped around business context, operating realities and long-term enterprise value.

Knowledge that stays with you
We work collaboratively so capability, understanding and decision-making remain within your organisation.

3. Add one distinctly Noeveka visual brand moment

Introduce one architectural or geometric imagery section that represents structure emerging from complexity, connected systems, precision, architecture, perspective and intelligent transformation.

Recommended placement: after the technology / expertise section or immediately before “Why Noeveka”.

Recommended desktop section height: approximately 700-850 px on a 1920 px wide canvas.

Use either a full-bleed visual or a 45-55% visual split with a concise statement.

Do not place the image inside another rounded card.

4. Add a point-of-view statement

Design direction: dark warm near-black background, white/off-white type, orange as a fine accent, headline around 40-52 px, and 100-140 px vertical padding.

5. Clarify the Ajay / architect-led expertise section

Section headline: Senior expertise stays close to the work.

Noeveka was built around a simple principle: complex enterprise decisions deserve experienced architectural judgement. Engagements remain architect-led, ensuring strategy, architecture and execution stay connected.

Identity: Ajay Kumar | Founder & CEO

Optional descriptor, if approved: Enterprise Data & AI Architect

Experience badge: 15+ years | Enterprise Data & Architecture Experience

Remove any 4.9 / 5 style rating unless it is sourced from a genuine, verifiable review platform.

6. Technology logo strip

Heading: Experience across leading enterprise platforms

Optional supporting line: Technology choices guided by enterprise fit, not vendor preference.

Keep vendor marks visually secondary. Target a consistent displayed logo height around 20-28 px on desktop.

7. Reduce card repetition

Retain cards for Services, Testimonials and the FAQ accordion where useful. Reduce card treatment for differentiators, numbered principles, point-of-view content and architectural statements.

8. Service section copy and treatment

Eyebrow: OUR CORE EXPERTISE

Heading: Expertise for complex enterprise data and AI decisions

The current three-card visual composition can remain. Be aware that the orange middle card reads as the featured service. Keep that emphasis only if it is intentional.

9. Testimonials

Heading: What clients say

Supporting copy: Experiences from enterprise leaders who have worked with Noeveka’s independent, architect-led approach.

10. Metrics section

Recommended display scale: large metric value 52-64 px on desktop, supporting description 13-15 px.

11. Body copy and typography hierarchy

Keep body copy at 16 px minimum on desktop and mobile. Use line-height around 1.5-1.65 and keep paragraph width around 600-680 px where possible.

12. Navigation and CTA refinements

Navigation logo: Increase approximately 10-15%. Target displayed height around 28-34 px depending on final SVG proportions.

Navigation CTA: Start a conversation →

Hero CTA: Book a Strategy Call →

13. Final CTA section

Headline: Ready to architect your enterprise’s data future?

Supporting copy: Start with a focused conversation about your current landscape, priorities and the decisions ahead.

Primary CTA: Book a Strategy Call →

Secondary CTA: Explore Our Expertise

Design: approximately 72-96 px vertical padding, panel max-width around 1100-1200 px, and enough breathing room for the section to read as the conclusion of the page.

14. Footer brand signoff

Retain the oversized faint NOEVEKA wordmark in the footer and increase its presence slightly. Aim for approximately 3-6% opacity so it is discovered rather than immediately dominant.

P2 - Polish and refinement

Section spacing

Major sections: approximately 96-128 px vertical spacing. Related subsections: 56-72 px. Card gaps: 20-24 px.

Card styling

Use restrained 10-16 px corner radius and very subtle shadows. Keep the visual language architectural and precise, not soft or app-like.

Orange usage

Use orange as a signal colour for CTAs, small markers, selected words, numbering, thin rules and micro-interactions. Avoid large orange surfaces outside intentional featured moments.

Motion

Use subtle fades, slight vertical reveals, gentle image movement and restrained number animation. Recommended transition duration: 350-600 ms. Avoid bouncing, dramatic zooming, excessive parallax, animated data streams, floating icons and cursor gimmicks.
