Executive implementation summary

P0 - Must resolve before launch

1. Hero proposition and supporting copy

Hero headline: Where Data Architecture Meets Human Judgment.

Keep the headline. It is distinctive, ownable, and correctly connects technical depth with senior judgement.

Supporting copy: We are an independent enterprise Data & AI advisory firm, built by architects who believe technology creates value only when it is shaped by sound judgement, clear architecture and business context.

Primary CTA: Start a Conversation →

Secondary CTA: Explore Focus Areas

2. Hero founder image

Use an editorial founder portrait rather than a profile-photo treatment. The image should feel like a founder portrait for a premium advisory firm, not a LinkedIn headshot or a technology sales image.

Ajay in a natural modern office or architectural environment.

Relaxed but senior posture, direct or slightly off-camera gaze.

No obvious “consultant pitching over a laptop” pose.

Preserve realistic skin texture and natural lighting.

Avoid futuristic AI graphics, holograms, floating interfaces or obvious technology clichés.

3. Validate all credibility claims

Before production launch, validate all metrics, dates, client statements, platform experience, geographic claims and experience figures. Dummy or placeholder credibility content must not reach the live site.

P2 - Polish / refinement

1. Increase the main content width

The current page feels slightly narrow on large desktop screens. Increase major two-column sections while preserving comfortable reading line lengths.

2. Hero spacing

Give the hero more opening presence. On desktop, target approximately 100-130 px between the navigation and the main hero content, subject to viewport height.

3. Typography scale

Do not reduce mobile body copy below 15-16 px simply to preserve the desktop composition.

4. Timeline treatment

Keep the large year numerals and two-column alignment.

Use a subtle 1 px vertical guide line at approximately 10-15% opacity.

Use orange only for milestone indicators or selected emphasis.

Keep the timeline feeling architectural and editorial rather than decorative.

5. Editorial eyebrow labels

Keep labels such as THE FRUSTRATION WE SAW, THE MOMENT WE REALISED and OUR JOURNEY only where they help narrative orientation. Standardise the treatment across the page.

Same font size and weight.

Same uppercase treatment and letter spacing.

Same orange colour treatment.

Same spacing above the main heading.

Creative direction summary

The About Us page is already one of the strongest parts of the site. The goal is not to add more content or components. The final pass should make the story feel less like a company biography and more like the origin of a clear advisory philosophy: independent judgement, architect-led thinking, practical transformation and measurable enterprise value.
